LadieBuggz is a women-centered empowerment, wellness, storytelling, and community brand. This policy explains, in plain terms, what information is involved when you visit this website — and what is not.

About this website

This website is informational. It does not offer user accounts, and it does not sell anything or take payments on the site itself. It links out to other platforms — such as our Facebook Page and shop, Instagram, WhatsApp catalog, and Amazon Storefront — where you can shop, subscribe, message us, or follow along. When you use those platforms, their own privacy policies govern your activity there.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information through this website. There are no sign-up forms, no accounts, and no checkout here.

  • Standard server logs: like nearly every website, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when your browser loads a page — for example, your IP address, browser type, the pages requested, and the date and time. These logs are kept by the hosting provider for security and operation of the service; we do not use them to identify or profile individual visitors.
  • If you contact us: if you email or message us, we receive the contact details and message content you choose to send, and we use them only to respond to you.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies or run analytics or advertising trackers of its own. The platforms we link to (such as Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Amazon) may use cookies and similar technologies under their own privacy and cookie policies, which you can review on those services.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not build profiles of this website's visitors.
  • We do not access any account on any platform without its owner's authorization.

Your choices

Because this site does not hold personal information about its visitors, there is usually nothing here to change or remove. If you have contacted us and would like that correspondence deleted, or if you have any privacy question at all, email us and we will help — see our Data Deletion page for how to make a request.
