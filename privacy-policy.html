Privacy Policy — LadieBuggz™ | Where Love Lives

Effective date: November 18, 2026
Operator: 3amiGlobal LLC DBA LadieBuggz ("LadieBuggz," "we")

LadieBuggz Ecosystem is a women-centered empowerment, wellness, storytelling, and community platform. This policy describes what information the LadieBuggz app and connected services collect, and how it is used.

Information we collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate connected social features:

  • Basic profile information from accounts you authorize (such as name and profile identifiers provided by Meta platforms)
  • Authorized Page and account data for the Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts you connect
  • Content performance metrics and audience insights for those connected accounts
  • Engagement activity on connected accounts (such as comments and interactions the app is authorized to manage)

How we use information

Information is used only to support:

  • Content publishing to your authorized Pages and accounts
  • Analytics and content performance review
  • Community engagement and comment management
  • Customer and community communication
  • Platform improvement

What we do not do

  • We do not sell user data.
  • We do not share user data with unauthorized third parties.
  • We do not scrape public content.
  • We do not access any account without its owner's authorization.

Data retention and deletion

Connected account data is retained only while the connection is active and needed for the features above. You may request deletion of your connected account data at any time — see our Data Deletion page: ladiebuggz.com/data-deletion
