Data Deletion — LadieBuggz™ | Where Love Lives

You may request deletion of your connected account data from the LadieBuggz Ecosystem at any time.

How to request deletion

Contact LadieBuggz support at LadieBuggz@ladiebuggz.com from the email associated with your connected account, with the subject line "Data Deletion Request," and tell us which Facebook Page or Instagram account the request covers.

What gets deleted

Upon a verified request, we remove from the LadieBuggz Ecosystem database:

  • Authorized account tokens for the connected account
  • Stored profile data for the connected account
  • Analytics references tied to the connected account
  • Stored connection records for the connected account

Timing

Requests are processed after we confirm the request comes from the account owner or an authorized admin of the connected Page or account. Deletion covers data stored by LadieBuggz Ecosystem; it does not delete your Facebook or Instagram accounts or content held by Meta, which remain under your control in those platforms.
