What we hold — and what we don't

The LadieBuggz website and app do not maintain user accounts, and we do not store personal data about visitors or followers on our own servers. This website is informational and holds no accounts, orders, or profiles.

Most information connected with LadieBuggz — your Facebook or Instagram profile, your comments and messages, your subscriptions and purchases — lives with the platform where it happened (for example, Meta for Facebook and Instagram, or WhatsApp, or Amazon), under that platform's own privacy policy and controls. We cannot delete data held by those platforms; only the platform can, at your direction through its settings.

How to request deletion

If you believe we hold any information associated with you — for example, an email or message you sent us — you may request its deletion at any time:

  • Email LadieBuggz@ladiebuggz.com
  • Use the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  • Identify the platform (for example, Facebook, Instagram, or email) and the username or address the request covers

We will acknowledge your request and process it within 30 days, deleting any records associated with you that are in our keeping.

Deleting your information on Meta platforms

For information held by Meta, you can review, download, or delete it directly in your Facebook and Instagram settings — including your activity information and off-platform activity controls in the Accounts Center and your Facebook information settings. Deleting or deactivating your account there is handled entirely by Meta.
